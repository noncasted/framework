## Findings and decisions

- No location rotation exists: the run always loads the scene `Game` (`Scenes.Game`, D2BlueRedX2Location inside). A stress
  location therefore means a stress scene.
- The fight never ends because `SwarmSimulation` moves the stage clock only while `_isSpawning`; `StopSpawning` each frame is the
  only change needed, RunStages and the encounter stay as they are (the director may spawn its pool for at most one frame after
  `StartStage`).
- `IPlayerLevelUp.Grant(ids)` takes each card once: "count" of `addUpgrade` means slots of a tree (root, left, right, capstone),
  a card by id/key is granted once. Elites and fallbacks are not in the random pool.
- Removing bodies: there is no "kill N" in `ISwarm`; a lower target is reached by the fight, `clearEnemies` uses `KillAll`.
  Invulnerability is always on (autorun), no API to switch it off. Particles of the VFX are not in `stats()` (no counter found).
- Mark names are `[a-z0-9._]`, so the marks are `stress.enemies.300`, not `stress.enemies[300]`.
- `context_meter --marker` alternated between two agent files (F54): its numbers were not reliable in this step.
