# 0.13.1

1 commit (`v0.13.0..d85670ca`): the sprite catalogue cuts clips out of a PNG strip. A PNG of the kind Multiple Animations describes in its userData
named clips (ranges and lists of frames, frames of a neighbouring strip of the same folder), the generator cuts them into nested objects of the group
without baking, like aseprite tags. The lines of the clips are edited in the inspector of the catalogue. `EditorCatalogues.Bind` binds
the generated catalogues to the assets of the project outside Play mode: for editor tools and EditMode tests. The game has nothing to do.

## Check the behaviour

- A PNG with `kind: MultipleAnimations` in its userData used to be generated as a plain strip (the kind worked only for aseprite). Now without
  `clips` the generation fails (`... is marked as Multiple Animations and has no clips`), as with a broken clip: no name, a repeated
  name, `time` <= 0, a frame outside the strip. Search: `git grep -l MultipleAnimations -- '*.png.meta'`. In the files found without `clips`
  change the kind to Animation or mark up the clips.
