# 0.18.3

3 commits (`v0.18.2..16b5cd43`):

- `framerate`: `FrameRateLimit` owns `QualitySettings.vSyncCount`. Unity ignores `Application.targetFrameRate` while `vSyncCount > 0`,
  so a quality level with vSync (Ultra of Standalone, High of WebGL in peace-data-reborn) hid FPS 30 / 60 and `?fps=`. A limit now sets
  `vSyncCount 0` and the target; the rate of the screen (auto on a desktop, `?fps=-1`) sets `vSyncCount 1` and the target `-1`: one
  frame a refresh, on the web one a `requestAnimationFrame`. Phones ignore `vSyncCount`. `settings.vsync` of the telemetry reports it.
- core: `IUpdater.RunPostUpdate(lifetime, Action<float>)`, an extension method over `PostUpdatableAction` (no change of `IUpdater`:
  the fakes of the games compile as they are).
- `deploy/registry.json`: mines-leader deploys on the monolith.

## Check the behaviour

- With `framerate`: Low / High (and `?fps=` on the web) give the chosen rate on every quality level, Standalone included; Auto on a desktop
  keeps the rate of the screen with vSync whatever the `vSyncCount` of the quality level. `settings.target_fps` and `settings.vsync` of
  the telemetry show the pair.
- A game that sets `QualitySettings.vSyncCount` itself or switches quality levels at runtime now fights the module: drop its own code,
  or re-apply after `SetQualityLevel`.
