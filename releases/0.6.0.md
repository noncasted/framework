# 0.6.0

6 commits (`v0.5.1..345586c`, one of them a merge). Console: the online counted by unique users (current online, the peak and the unique users
by hours and days, the page `/online`), Home without the infrastructure cards (they are only on `/infrastructure`), `HomeInfrastructureSection`
removed (BREAKING). Also: the number of connection attempts to Postgres is configurable, `PeerMesh` no longer loses the offer of a peer that came before the start
of the mesh, the hooks `preflight` / `beforeUpload` of `deploy-client`.

### 1. Console: online statistics, Home without the infrastructure cards (BREAKING)

Who: the backend and the console of every game. `HomeInfrastructureSection` is removed (its cards are now only on `/infrastructure`).

Search: `git grep -nE 'HomeInfrastructureSection|ConnectedUsersWidget|SetupCoordinator|ConnectedUsersLiveData' -- 'backend/*'`

- The build will not fail: Razor gives only the warning `RZ10012` on an unknown element and draws an empty tag, Home becomes empty. Look for it
  with the search above or `RZ10012` in the build log.
- The online count (`backend/Cluster/Monitoring/Online`): in `SetupCoordinator` (`Orchestration/Extensions/ProjectsSetupExtensions.cs`) call
  `builder.AddOnlineTracker()`, on the coordinator only. Source: `ConnectedUsersLiveData`, which the game already writes; a user is counted
  by id (two tabs or two gateways = 1). Read it on any host: `IDeploymentState<OnlineStatsLiveData>` (registered by `AddMonitoring`).
  A game that does not write `ConnectedUsersLiveData` (storytale-workshop) gets zero; the tracker and the widget are optional for it.
- Tests of the game that list the states of the framework get two new ones: `Cluster.Monitoring.Online.OnlineDayState` (`state_online_days`,
  `online_day`, key `Integer`) and `Cluster.Monitoring.Online.OnlineStatsLiveData` (`cluster`, `deployment_state_OnlineStatsLiveData`,
  key `Guid`, deploy-scoped). `Tools/Tests/Codegen/StatesLookupGeneratorTests.cs` (`_productionStates`; fails in pixel-swarm and
  storytale-workshop, checked), `Tools/Tests/Cluster/DeployCleanupTests.cs` (the list of deploy-scoped types and their identity, as for
  `ConnectedUsersLiveData`; pixel-swarm, peace-data-reborn, mines-leader). Example: `tools/framework/backend/Tests/Cluster/DeployCleanupTests.cs`.
- Home of the console (`Console/Home/HomeSections.razor`, `Home.razor`): `<HomeInfrastructureSection />` -> `<HomeClusterSection />` (Features and
  Client Telemetry, the latter only with `AddClientTelemetryReader`); remove the own `ConnectedUsersWidget`, put `<OnlineWidget />` in its place
  (current online, the peak and the unique users by hours / days, a click on the title opens `/online`). The other cards, the cards of the game
  (`AddConsoleInfrastructureCard`) and the new `Online` are on `/infrastructure`. `ConsoleInfrastructureCard` moved to `Console/Infrastructure`,
  the namespace is the same (`Console`). The comment in `GameModule.cs` about `ConnectedUsersWidget` and the registration made for it (`IUserCollection`
  for the names): remove them if nobody else needs them.
- The page `/online` (`ConsoleConstants.Pages.Online`) comes from the assembly `Console`, nothing to register. Tailwind classes: only
  the ones already in the `app.css` of every game, no need to rebuild `app.css`.

## Check the behaviour

- Home of the console: `HomeClusterSection` (Features, Client Telemetry) on the left, `OnlineWidget` on the right. The Home of the game already has its own
  `FeaturesStatusWidget` (all four games): Features shows up twice, remove the extra one as you like.
- `/infrastructure`: the cards of the game before the cards of the framework, the new `Online` among them.
- Postgres: the connection as before, 10 attempts once a second; after the last one the exception names the number of attempts (before: an empty `Exception`).
- `PeerMesh`: a player who started the mesh later than their join (a late player) now gets a direct connection, not a tunnel after a timeout.

## No action

- Postgres: `PostgresConnect:Attempts` and `PostgresConnect:RetryDelayMs` of the configuration (10 and 1000 by default), fewer than 1 attempt throws;
  storytale-workshop already sets `PostgresConnect__Attempts=1` in `PostResourcesSetupTests`.
- `network`: `PeerSignaling` keeps the last negotiation of a peer without a channel (the offer and the signals after it) until the first subscriber of a channel to
  it; the test `OfferBeforeMeshRun_Direct`. The API did not change.
- `deploy-client`: the optional hooks `preflight` (before the snapshot, also with `--notest`, may regenerate a file of the tree) and `beforeUpload`
  (after the builds, before the first upload, not with `--no-deploy`) in `.claude/deploy-client.json`; a failed hook stops the run. Source:
  pixel-swarm, already set up. Arrives through the link to the skill; the copies of the skill in mines-leader and peace-data-reborn do not know them.
- `split-commit`: commits right after the plan, without a confirmation. Arrives through the link; the copies in mines-leader and peace-data-reborn are old.
