# 0.2.0

49 commits (`v0.1.0..dad3907`). Fixed: losses in channels and pipes, the rollback and the takeover of transactions, a failed start of a lobby match, the loss of the close frame
when a connection closes and the leaks of WebRTC. The backend has no files longer than 300 lines, the whole test suite of the framework runs in parallel. The copies of `control` no longer
lose the work of an agent. The games have almost nothing to change in their working code: the changes touch the backend tests, `.gitignore` and `control`.

### 9. `ConnectionWriter.Run` gets the lifetime of the socket

Who: backend, code or tests that run `ConnectionWriter` by hand; nothing to change through `Connection`. Matches: `backend/Tools/Tests/Network/ConnectionWriterTests.cs` in mines-leader and peace-data-reborn, 8 calls each.

Search: `git grep -nE 'riter\.Run\(' -- 'backend/*.cs'`

Do: `writer.Run(lifetime)` -> `writer.Run(lifetime, lifetime)`. The second argument cancels a send in flight, which tears the socket down: `Connection` passes a lifetime that ends after the close frame, so a client reading at the moment of the close gets the message and the close, not a drop (1006). A hung send to a client that does not read is still limited by the close timeout.

## Check the behaviour

The compiler does not show this.

- Channel: a subscriber that has not received a single message gets a gap (`onGapDetected`) on a reactivation of the grain of the channel and rereads the state.
- The resubscription runs in parallel over the listeners, a pass does not wait for one listener longer than `RuntimeChannelOptions.ResubscribeTimeoutMs` (30000).
- `IRuntimeChannel.AddObserver`: the signature of the grain method changed; old and new nodes in a rolling deploy are probably incompatible, a conclusion from the code, not checked on a live cluster.
- `[Transaction]` on a target that is not `IGrainBase` fails.
- `IGrainTransactionHandler.OnFailure` collects the failures of the participants into an `AggregateException`. A grain whose rollback failed stays held: its memory is unknown, the takeover repeats the rollback.
- `Connection.Run` waits for the end of the close, also when the parent lifetime ended before `Run`. A client that does not answer the close is cut off by the close timeout, 2 s.
- A generic root function of the container used to compile, now it is `CINGR016`. The games have none, no step.
- WebRTC (`WebRtcPeerSocket`): the candidates of the offer go out after the answer is set, one signalling exchange later. A signal that a closed
  peer did not take is now a lost connection: `Connect` falls to the fallback path with a warning, no exception in the signalling code.
- The errors of `libdatachannel` are written as `Warning`, `Error` stays only for `Fatal`. Whoever looked for them in the log as errors no longer sees them.
- `LocalizedLabel`: a label destroyed before its lifetime throws `InvalidOperationException` on a change of the language (before, it skipped silently). An error in the log means the subscription lives longer than the label: pass a shorter lifetime. The other handlers of the language change keep working.
- `DesignButton` without an assigned `_button` fails in `OnEnable` (before, it looked for a `Button` at run time), `DesignLayoutBase.SetPadding(null)` fails in `Rebuild` (before, a zero padding). The `OnValidate` of the button now fills `_button` even if `_element` is assigned.
