# 0.9.2

silently went into the assets of the bundle with the scenes, and the second bundle (for example, a preloaded root through a lazy target it took) started to
depend on it - without a duplicate in `Inspect` and without an error. Now it is a duplicate: `Inspect` shows it, `ContentSharedSync` puts it into a shared
bundle, without the sync the build fails.

## Steps

### 1. Content: an asset needed by the assets and the scenes of one bundle and by another bundle is a duplicate
Who: the client, games with a `ContentBundle` that has both assets and scenes.
Search: no general one; after the sync of the shared bundles (Framework > Codegen > Sync Shared Content Bundles) or in the build error
`... is needed by the content bundles ...` there are assets that were not among the duplicates before.
Do: after the update resync the shared bundles (Framework > Codegen > Sync Shared Content Bundles), `ContentSharedSync` puts
such assets into a shared bundle by `IContentSharedBundles`. If a preloaded root holds assets added by hand because of a duplicate with the scenes
of another bundle (so that the root does not pull the bundle with the scenes), remove them from the roots and resync again. Check in the `[ContentBuild]` log
that the `dependencies` of the root have no bundle with scenes.

## Check the behaviour

A build that silently gave a dependency of a root (or of another bundle) on a bundle with scenes now fails with the duplicate error
`... is needed by the content bundles ...` until the shared bundles are resynced. After the update of the pointer without a resync the content build
may fail - this is expected, step 1 fixes it. After the resync `Inspect` shows new duplicates, and in `[ContentBuild]` the root loses
its dependency on the bundle with scenes.
